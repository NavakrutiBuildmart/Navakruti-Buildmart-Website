About Navakruti

One place for your building and finishing needs.

Navakruti Buildmart is a building materials showroom based in Ambegaon Budruk, Pune.

We bring together tiles, sanitaryware, CP fittings, kitchen sinks, construction chemicals, adhesives, bath accessories and other building and finishing solutions.

Our aim is to make product selection simpler for homeowners, contractors, architects, designers and customers working on construction and renovation projects.
